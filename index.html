Souno Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

Introduction

Souno ("we", "our", "the app") is an ambient sound mixing application. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and encrypted password through Firebase Authentication. We do not store your password in plaintext.

Usage Data

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data including:

  • Which sounds are played (aggregated, not tied to your identity)
  • Feature usage (timer, presets, favorites)
  • App crashes and performance metrics

This data helps us improve the app and is not shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Preferences

Sound preferences, favorites, and presets are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app
  • To manage your account and subscription
  • To improve app features based on aggregated usage patterns
  • To send bedtime reminders (only if you enable them)

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication — account management (Google LLC)
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage analytics (Google LLC)
  • Firebase App Check — API abuse prevention (Google LLC)
  • Google Play Billing — subscription payments (Google LLC)

These services have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

Account data is retained until you delete your account. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen in the app. Upon deletion, your email and authentication data are permanently removed from Firebase.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all network communication
  • Firebase Authentication with hashed passwords
  • No cleartext traffic permitted
  • App Check to prevent unauthorized API access

Children's Privacy

Souno is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your account information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of analytics (by not creating an account)
  • Disable bedtime notifications

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
